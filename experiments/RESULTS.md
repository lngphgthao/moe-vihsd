# Kết quả nghiên cứu

Đây là lớp diễn giải cho nguồn sự thật machine-readable:
`experiments/experiment_tracker.csv`.

The tracker records one experiment/run per row. This file should contain concise observations,
interpretations, hypotheses, and confirmed findings. It must not invent missing metrics or turn
planned experiments into completed results.

## Trạng thái hiện tại

The tracker contains reported ViANLI dense, Transformer-MoE, Classifier-MoE, and ViHSD entries
migrated from the previous results ledger. Their `verification` field is `unverified` until the
resolved configuration and saved metrics are checked.

## Quan sát ban đầu

- Dense ViANLI backbone runs are available for XLM-R-base, PhoBERT-base, and mBERT across
  seeds 42-44.
- Most listed ViANLI MoE comparisons are single-seed records, so they are not confirmation of
  a stable architecture effect.
- The listed Classifier-MoE ViANLI result is substantially lower than the dense baselines and
  should be treated as a collapse/anomaly investigation target, not as a final conclusion.
- The available Hybrid-MoE run metrics show validation Macro-F1 37.51, training contradiction
  F1 0.079, and auxiliary loss 23.12 versus task loss 1.01. Its tracker status is `anomaly`;
  configuration and checkpoint artifacts are missing, so the cause is not verified. Its
  historical test score was generated by the former automatic training evaluation and is
  retained for provenance only, not selection.
- ViHSD includes historical test values, but those values must not guide new selection until
  their finalist status and provenance are verified.

These are observations of recorded rows, not confirmed causal findings.

## Format diễn giải

For each meaningful update, use four explicit labels:

- **Observation:** what the tracker directly records.
- **Interpretation:** the most defensible explanation currently available.
- **Hypothesis:** a proposed explanation that still needs testing.
- **Confirmed finding:** a conclusion supported by controlled comparisons and repeated seeds.

## Câu hỏi hiện tại

1. Are the dense ViANLI backbone differences larger than seed variation?
2. Does Transformer-MoE improve validation macro F1 under matched settings and parameter budget?
3. Is the Classifier-MoE result caused by label mapping, loss behavior, routing collapse, or
   representation/classifier dynamics?
4. Does a placement or routing effect transfer across ViANLI and ViHSD?
5. Which candidates remain competitive after efficiency and multi-seed confirmation?

## Policy cập nhật

When a new result is provided externally:

1. Validate its metadata and metric names.
2. Add or update the corresponding tracker row without deleting history.
3. Record provenance and verification status.
4. Update this file with a concise interpretation.
5. Update `analysis_notes.md` when a hypothesis changes.
